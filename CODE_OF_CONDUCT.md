# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Discuss evidence and wording rather than attacking people
- Credit creators and accept good-faith corrections
- Avoid publishing private, harmful, or deceptive material

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private security contact path rather than a public issue
